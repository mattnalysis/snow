Landings VII and VIII HOA Board meeting Minutes

Jan 15 2023| 1:00 PM | Meeting called by Mike Ulrey, Treasurer

Nuh Aydin, Cindy Cunningham**, Mark Long*, Matt Snow, Mike Ulrey, Andrea White

(*not present;**left early)

Agenda
  • Welcome
  • Annual HOA meeting: when and where?
  • Topics for annual HOA meeting.
  • How to recruit new board of trustee (BOT) members and officers of the BOT.
  • Parliamentarian for annual HOA meeting.
Proceedings
  • After a short discussion, it was decided to schedule the annual HOA meeting for Sunday Feb 19, 2023 at 3:00 PM. The Station Break in Mt Vernon is the suggested location. Mike Ulrey will contact The Station Break for availability. Andrea White suggested we try to arrange a simultaneous Zoom meeting for those who prefer to attend virtually.
  • Topics for the HOA meeting include:
  • Nominate and elect members for the board of trustees.
  • Treasurer’s report (Mike Ulrey)
  • A brief, up-to-date synopsis of the pending legal case concerning the dam/lake. It was agreed that this is NOT the main concern of the annual HOA meeting, which is mainly to conduct ordinary business of the HOA. However, it is important to keep the HOA duly informed about the legal proceedings already being handled separately by our lawyers. This should be short but sufficiently informative to stave off an endless discussion. This would serve to summarize what Mark Long already provides us in his timely emails with legal documents attached.
  • We still do not have any volunteers for president, secretary, or treasurer in 2023. We also do not have any concrete plans (or methodology) to recruit said individuals. Everyone informally agreed (sort of) to talk to neighbors and/or get the word out that we are looking for volunteers to step forward. Mike Ulrey will talk to Mark Long concerning the email address list he has compiled to communicate with the HOA concerning our legal issue with the dam/lake. We should be able to utilize this list to get the word out about the annual HOA meeting AND the need for new board of trustee members AND officers.
  • We need someone to impose parliamentarian procedures during our February HOA meeting, to keep things orderly. It looks like Mike Ulrey will be running the meeting (unless someone objects and is willing to step up!), and he will need help imposing Robert’s Rules of Order.

Mike Ulrey

Treasurer, Landings Phase VII and VIII HOA

(and completely unofficially, acting president and secretary)
