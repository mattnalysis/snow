IN THE COURT OF COMMON PLEAS
KNOX COUNTY, OHIO

PROPOSED ORDER GRANTING JOINT
MOTION FOR EXTENSION OF DISPOSITIVE MOTION DEADLINE 

This matter comes before the Court on Long Defendants, Mullins Bros. Ltd., and the State of Ohio ex rel., Dave Yost, Ohio Attorney Generals’ Joint Motion for Extension of the Dispositive Motion Deadline. Having considered the arguments of these Parties, and for good cause shown, the motion is hereby GRANTED. Defendants shall have through December 1, 2023 to file any dispositive motions. Memoranda contra to those motions may be filed through December 29, 2023. Reply memoranda in support of those motions may be filed through January 5, 2024.
IT IS SO ORDERED.
