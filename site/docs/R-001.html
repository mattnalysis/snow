Landings VII and VIII HOA Board meeting Minutes

Nov 13 2022| 1:00 PM | Meeting called by Mike Ulrey, Treasurer

Nuh Aydin, Cindy Cunningham, Mark Long, Matt Snow, Mike Ulrey, Andrea White*

(*not present)

Agenda
  • Welcome
  • Discussion of viability of HOA and consequences of dissolution
  • Depending on conclusions,
  • If dissolution seems to be the consensus, what are the consequences and how do we plan for it?
  • If maintaining the HOA seems to be the consensus, how do we invigorate the HOA, that is, elect new officers and get our meetings back on schedule per the covenants and by-laws?
  • Adjourn

Mark Long, who has been the liaison between the lawyers (Eastman and Smith, in particular Steve Chappalear) pointed out that dissolving the HOA at this time might be perceived as a ruse to escape some sort of financial penalty involving the dam issue, even if it was done for a completely unrelated reason. Nuh Aydin pointed out in last February’s general HOA meeting that, although some people were in favor of dissolution, many were not, not to mention that many did not express an opinion.

As a result it was decided to put off any (immediate) thoughts of dissolution and instead to take steps to get the HOA back on a normal track. This includes scheduling a no-later-than Feb 28, 2023 full HOA meeting, according to the covenants and by-laws. The purpose will be to bring everyone up to date on current developments (e.g. the dam issue), but also to elect Board members (also called “Directors” in the Covenants).

In preparation for this, the 6 current Board members will meet on Sunday Jan, 8 2023 at 1:00 PM. The main purpose will be to nominate candidates for the Board. In the meantime, we will be looking for willing candidates for the Board in general, and in particular any who are interested in serving as president, secretary or treasurer once they are on the Board.

After the February general all-members HOA meeting, the (possibly new Board) will meet a short time later to elect “Officers” of the Board, meaning president, secretary, and treasurer (and assistants to these if desired).

Mike Ulrey

Treasurer, Landings Phase VII and VIII HOA
